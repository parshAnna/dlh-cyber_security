# 1x03 — Defense Blueprint

**Organization:** MedDefense Health Systems  
**Program:** DLH Cybersecurity — Blue Team  
**Planning horizon:** Six months  
**Security investment ceiling:** $120,000

## Purpose
Translate the asset and security posture assessment (1x00), threat landscape analysis (1x01), and vulnerability findings (1x02) into a documented, risk-prioritized security strategy.

## Deliverables
- [Task 0 — Framework Landscape](0-framework_landscape.md): Comparison of NIST CSF 2.0, CIS Controls v8 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022, with a MedDefense framework recommendation.
- [Task 1 — NIST CSF 2.0 Current Profile](1-nist_csf_mapping.md): Six functions, current maturity, supporting evidence, key gaps, and proposed six-month target maturity.

- [Task 2 — CIS Controls Audit](2-cis_controls_audit.md): Assessment of all 18 CIS Controls, maturity scorecard, and five prioritized controls.
- [Task 3 — Gap-to-Framework Bridge](3-gap_framework_bridge.md): Eight prioritized gaps mapped to vulnerabilities, threat kill chains, NIST CSF functions, CIS Controls, and recommended actions.
- [Task 4 — Governance Architecture](4-governance_architecture.md): RACI matrix, data governance roles, and CISO/vCISO recommendation.
- [Task 5 — Risk Equation](5-risk_equation.md): Five quantitative risk scenarios with AV, EF, SLE, ARO, ALE, confidence, and sensitivity analysis.
- [Task 6 — ALE Workshop](6-ale_workshop.md): Five MedDefense risks, baseline and residual ALE, proposed controls, economic comparison, and risk ranking.
- [Task 7 — Cost-Benefit Analysis](7-cost_benefit_analysis.md): Eight security controls assessed by annual cost, ALE reduction, net value, and budget fit.

## Evidence and governance approach
- 1x00 — Asset Registry, Criticality Matrix, Control Inventory and Gap Analysis.
- 1x01 — Threat actors, attack vectors and mapped kill chains.
- 1x02 — Vulnerability findings, triage, contextual risk and validation plans.

Analyst-produced assessments are not proof of implemented controls. Proposed controls must be distinguished from operationally verified controls. Risk claims must be traceable to the three projects, and quantified recommendations must show their assumptions and calculations.

## Constraints
- Total recommended investment must not exceed **$120,000**.
- Patient-care continuity and medical-device safety must be preserved.
- All deliverables must use professional English, cite evidence, and end with a newline.

## Status
Task 1 evidence review completed against Projects 1x00, 1x01 and 1x02. Current maturity ratings remain evidence-based assessments; six-month targets are proposed and subject to implementation and validation.
