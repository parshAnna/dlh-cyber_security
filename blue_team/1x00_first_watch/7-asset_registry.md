**Status conventions:** Active means a reported scan response or explicit operational evidence in the supplied artifacts, not current health, approval or supportability. Unknown means current operation or identity cannot be established. Shadow IT (unmanaged) is used for the personal laptop and provisionally for the two explicitly undocumented responding devices; their ownership and authorization need confirmation. Deprecated requires retirement/deprecation evidence and is not assigned solely because an OS is old or unsupported. Formal departmental ownership is not supplied for most assets; suggested validation departments are labeled “proposed,” not represented as assigned owners.

**Network conventions:** The /24 labels reproduce the scan's grouping, not verified security boundaries. S explicitly states Central's 10.10.1/2/3 address groups share a physical network without VLAN/firewall separation; O reports a flat 10.10.0.0/16. Westside and HQ are reached through VPNs. OS values in responding-device rows are scan detections, not authoritative installed-version checks. Open ports do not establish application identity unless the source names the service. Individual scan rows, logical applications, physical assets and aggregate platforms are distinguished; their combined count is not a count of unique physical machines.
